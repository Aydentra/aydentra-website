CASE STUDY #001 / FINANCE AUTOMATION

Accounts receivable management.

From manual account and invoice review to an automated workflow based on business rules.

01

The challenge

Reviewing accounts and invoices requires interpreting credit terms, evaluating conditions and handling exceptions before taking action.

02

The approach

A workflow that connects information review with business rules, validations and controlled actions.

03

The controls

Exception handling and condition validation to maintain control over each process decision.

A process with decisions, not just tasks.

  1. Review accounts and invoices
  2. Interpret credit terms
  3. Evaluate rules and conditions
  4. Handle exceptions
  5. Execute controlled actions

An anonymized case. Companies, customers, users and internal systems are omitted. Savings and ROI figures are not published without validation.
